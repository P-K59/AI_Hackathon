How It Works

Your 3-Step Journey

We made the registration quick and effortless so you can focus on creativity, not paperwork.

01

Share Your Idea

Fill out a 5-minute form with 12 required answers. No finished code or prototype needed—just tell us what problem you noticed.

02

We Review & Shortlist

Faculty mentors and AI researchers evaluate submissions on novelty, community importance, and early feasibility to select top finalist teams.

03

Final Hackathon Round

Shortlisted teams participate in the final 2-day hackathon (October 15–16, 2026) with expert mentorship, computational support, and Best Innovation awards.

Focus Areas

Thematic Hackathon Tracks

Choose the area where you want to make an impact. Each track is tailored for real Indian challenges.

Social Impact

AI for Social Impact

Solutions addressing accessibility, regional education, citizen services, sanitation, and empowerment of underserved communities.

Example Idea: Voice assistant in local languages helping village elders apply for pension schemes.
Healthcare

AI in Healthcare

Early diagnostic aids for primary clinics, rural telemedicine support, assistive health devices, and maternal healthcare intelligence.

Example Idea: Smartphone camera filter detecting early eye disorders in remote health camps.
Agriculture

AI in Agriculture & Rural Dev

Crop disease detection, soil moisture optimization, weather alerts, transparent mandi pricing, and pest mitigation tools.

Example Idea: An offline app that scans crop leaf photos and guides farmers in Hindi or Marathi.
Cyber & Defence

AI in Cyber Security & Defence

Real-time threat analytics, digital identity protection, deepfake fraud prevention, and critical network infrastructure safeguards.

Example Idea: Lightweight scanner alerting users when incoming audio calls use synthetic cloned voices.

Our Open Door Inclusivity Promise

Innovation belongs to everyone. The hackathon is designed to be accessible to all students across India:

  • Any academic branch (IT & Non-IT)
  • Any recognized college or university
  • No prior code or prototype required
  • No GitHub or technical profile needed
  • Zero registration fees
  • Cross-college teams welcome
